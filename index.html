HomeFunds Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

HomeFunds is designed to help users manage household finances, including income, expenses, budgets, accounts, recurring transactions, financial records, shared household data, backups, and optional data import and export features.

1. Information We Collect

HomeFunds may process the following information when you use the app:

  • Financial information that you enter into the app, such as accounts, balances, income, expenses, categories, budgets, notes, and transaction records.
  • Household information and shared financial data used when you join or manage a HomeFunds household.
  • Your Google account email address when you choose to use Google sign-in or Google services required for features such as household joining, backup, restore, sync, or data export.
  • Data that you choose to import from or export to supported services, such as Google Drive or Google Sheets.

2. How Your Information Is Used

HomeFunds uses your information only to provide the app's features, including:

  • Managing your personal or household financial records.
  • Creating, restoring, and synchronizing backups when you choose to use those features.
  • Supporting shared household access and invitations.
  • Importing or exporting financial data when you request it.
  • Providing optional notifications and recurring transaction functionality.

3. Local Data Storage

HomeFunds is designed as a local-first application. Your financial data is stored on your device for the core functionality of the app.

4. Google Drive, Google Account, and Google Services

Some HomeFunds features use Google services only when you choose to connect and authorize your Google account.

Depending on the feature you use, HomeFunds may request access to:

  • Your Google account identity information, such as your email address.
  • Google Drive files required for HomeFunds backup, restore, sync, or shared household functionality.
  • Google Sheets when you choose to export or otherwise use a feature that requires Sheets access.

Google access tokens are used to perform the actions that you explicitly authorize. HomeFunds does not sell your Google account information.

5. Backups and Shared Household Data

If you use Google Drive backup, restore, sync, or shared household features, relevant HomeFunds data may be stored or accessed through your authorized Google Drive account so that the requested feature can function. You control whether to use these features by connecting and authorizing your Google account.

6. Data Sharing

HomeFunds does not sell your personal or financial information. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

Your data may be shared only as necessary to provide features that you explicitly use, for example with members of a shared HomeFunds household or with Google services that you authorize for backup, sync, restore, or export.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect your information using reasonable technical measures. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete HomeFunds data stored on your device by deleting the relevant data within the app or uninstalling the app, subject to your device and operating system behavior. Data stored in your own Google Drive through HomeFunds backup or sync features remains under your Google account and can be managed or deleted from Google Drive.

9. Children's Privacy

HomeFunds is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published at this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
